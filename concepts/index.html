Redesign concepts

Three playable directions for the rare-answer trivia game. Each has its own pixel-art world and its own rules. All three use the real question bank.

CONCEPT A · 16-BIT EARTH TONES

MOTHERLODE

Dig a mine shaft. Every answer swings the pick.

  • 3 prompts, 30 seconds each
  • Name as many answers as you can
  • Rarer answers dig deeper; deep cuts leave gems
  • Score is your depth in metres
 CONCEPT B · NEON SPACE

LIFTOFF

Launch a rocket. Rare answers boost you past the moon.

  • 5 prompts, one answer each
  • 3 fuel cells: wrong guesses burn them
  • Chain rare answers for a slingshot multiplier
  • Score is your altitude in km
 CONCEPT C · FOUR-COLOR STORYBOOK

QUILL DUEL

A turn-based battle. Your answer is your attack.

  • Same prompt for you and a rival
  • Rarity is damage
  • Matching answers clash: no damage
  • Knock out the rival or lead on HP
