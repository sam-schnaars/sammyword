## Description [4000]

Sammy Word puts a daily game in your group chat.

Open it from the + menu in any Messages conversation and pick a game. Finish a round and your result drops into the chat as a picture bubble, so everyone can see how you did and take their turn.

OUR GAMES
• Caption the Meme: one famous old painting or space photo a day, the same for everyone. Write the funniest caption, then see everyone else's and vote for the best.
• Scroll Recall: scroll through 8 short NASA space reels, then answer 5 questions about what you saw.
• Word Hunt: today's letter grid. Drag across touching letters to spell as many words as you can in 30 seconds.
• Flag Match: today's 5 flags, one minute. Name each country.

MORE DAILY GAMES
• Magnitudle and 100 of Us play right in the Sammy Word app, with their creators' permission. When you finish, your score is ready to send to a chat.
• Krillion and Eraly open on their creators' websites.

WHAT YOU GET
• A featured game every day, plus a streak for the days you play
• Score bubbles anyone in the chat can tap to play the same game
• A pixel-art look, with no account or sign-up

Captions are moderated: slurs and sexual terms are filtered, and you can report any caption. The daily games from other creators are theirs, and Sammy Word isn't affiliated with them.

## App Review notes

Sammy Word is an iMessage app. To test it:

1. Open Messages and start a conversation (any contact).
2. Tap + next to the text field, then More, then Sammy Word.
3. Play Word Hunt, Flag Match or Scroll Recall: when the round ends, the result is added to the conversation as an interactive message.
4. Caption the Meme: write a caption and submit it to today's shared board, then vote for another player's caption. Captions are filtered for slurs and sexual terms; any caption can be reported (3 reports hide it, and we review reports), and you can hide a person's captions from your own view. The rules and a contact link are shown above the Submit button.

New in 1.1:
- Magnitudle (magnitudle.com) and 100 of Us (logicers.com) are now played inside the Sammy Word app, not inside the iMessage extension, because the extension can't show ads. Their creators gave written permission for their games, including their ads, to be shown in Sammy Word (emails dated Nov 24 and Nov 26, 2026; available on request). The extension's "Play in Sammy Word" button opens the app, which shows the site; links that leave the site, including ad clicks, open in Safari. When a round finishes, the app offers a Messages sheet with the score.
- Krillion and Eraly still just open their websites in Safari.
- Caption the Meme uses public-domain images (classic paintings and NASA photos) from Wikimedia Commons.
- Scroll Recall uses public-domain NASA video.

No account or login is required.
