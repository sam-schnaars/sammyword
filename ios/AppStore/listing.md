## Description [4000]

Sammy Word brings a daily game to your group chat.

Open it from the + menu in any Messages conversation. There's a game of the day, plus three more you can play anytime. Finish a round and your score drops into the chat as a picture bubble, so everyone can see how you did and take their turn.

TODAY'S GAMES
• Krillion: a daily dive where the rarer your answers, the bigger your score.
• Magnitudle: one mind-bending number a day. Guess it and get scored out of 100.
• 100 of Us: of 100 people in the world, how many…? Get as close as you can.
• Eraly: seven moments from history. Guess the year of each, for up to 700 points.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open a chat in Messages.
2. Tap +, then More, and pick Sammy Word.
3. Hit PLAY. When you finish, your score goes into the chat.

WHAT YOU GET
• A featured game every day, in turn
• A streak counter for the days you play
• Score bubbles anyone in the chat can tap to play the same game
• A pixel-art look, with no account or sign-up

The daily games are made by their own creators and open inside the app from their websites. Sammy Word isn't affiliated with them. Sammy Word doesn't collect your personal data; the game sites may show their own ads.

## App Review notes

Sammy Word is an iMessage app. To test it:

1. Open Messages and start a conversation (any contact).
2. Tap + next to the text field, then More, then Sammy Word.
3. Tap PLAY on the game of the day and finish a round. The score is added to the conversation as an interactive message.

The companion iPhone app explains these steps and has an Open Messages button.

The four daily games (Krillion, Magnitudle, 100 of Us, Eraly) are third-party websites shown in a web view inside the app. The web view stays on each game's own site: links and ads that lead elsewhere don't open. We emailed each game's creator on November 23, 2026 to ask for permission and are waiting for replies. If any creator declines, we'll remove that game in an update.

No account or login is required.
