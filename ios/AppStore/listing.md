## Description [4000]

Sammy Word puts games right inside your group chat.

Open it from the + menu in any Messages conversation and pick a game. Finish a round and your score drops into the chat as a picture bubble, so everyone can see how you did and take their turn.

THE GAMES
• Doodle: pick a secret word, draw it on a little pixel grid and send it. Your friends get six guesses, with letters filling in as hints. Whoever guesses draws next.
• Word Hunt: today's letter grid. Drag across touching letters to spell as many words as you can in 80 seconds. Everyone gets the same board, so your score is one to beat.
• Flag Match: today's 12 flags, one minute. Name each country before time runs out.

MORE DAILY GAMES
Links to Krillion, Magnitudle, 100 of Us and Eraly, which open on their creators' websites. When you've played, paste or type your score to share it in the chat.

WHAT YOU GET
• A featured game every day, plus a streak for the days you play
• Score bubbles anyone in the chat can tap to play the same game
• A pixel-art look, with no account or sign-up

Sammy Word doesn't collect your personal data. The linked daily games are made by their own creators and Sammy Word isn't affiliated with them.

## App Review notes

Sammy Word is an iMessage app. To test it:

1. Open Messages and start a conversation (any contact).
2. Tap + next to the text field, then More, then Sammy Word.
3. Play Word Hunt or Flag Match: when the round ends, the score is added to the conversation as an interactive message.
4. Play Doodle: pick a word, draw it and tap Send Doodle. The other person taps the bubble to guess it.

The companion iPhone app explains these steps and has an Open Messages button.

Word Hunt, Flag Match and Doodle are Sammy Word's own games. The app also links to four daily games made by others (Krillion, Magnitudle, 100 of Us, Eraly). These are not shown inside the app: tapping one opens the creator's website in Safari, and the player can then type or paste their score to share it.

No account or login is required.
